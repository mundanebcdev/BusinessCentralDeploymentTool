# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project is a small technical community. The expectations are simple:

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a contribution, and explain your reasoning
  when you disagree rather than escalating.
- Do not post other people's private information, tenant identifiers, customer
  data, or credentials.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory
language, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space where you
are representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/mundanebcdev). Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy, and may
temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for repeated or severe violations.
